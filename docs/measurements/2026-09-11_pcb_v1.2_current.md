# PCB v1.2 current: sleep, BLE and a full logging cycle — 2026-09-11

First power measurement on the **assembled PCB v1.2** (everything before this was the
breadboard). One uninterrupted hour with the logger measuring every 5 minutes.

Setup: PCB v1.2, TFmini Plus on the switched rail, **four used Energizer L91 cells**
(6.31 V at rest, no bench supply), **picowatt** (INA228, 15 mΩ) in the supply line,
`--mode single --adcrange 1 --preset quiet` (118.8 Sa/s) with `--cal`, so the stored zero
offset (−81.0 µA, range 1) is applied per sample and no difference method is needed.
Firmware: TFmini build of `295adc8` + the −Y tilt axis (`SNOWGAUGE_SENSOR_AXIS_NEG_Y`),
schedule all day / 5 min, clock synced, no phone connected except where noted.

The breadboard numbers it replaces: sleep 32 µA ± 10 and 0.037 mAh per cycle

## Two things the hour taught us

**1. The reset backoff delayed the first measurement by more than half an hour.** The first
attempt at this measurement recorded no cycles at all for 26 minutes. Cause: the
consecutive-reset counter (NVS `sgd/boots`) had been driven up by power-cycling the board
while wiring the meter, and the scheduler holds the first measurement off by
`CONFIG_SNOWGAUGE_BOOT_HOLDOFF_MIN` (10 min) × that count, halting measurements entirely at
12. After one clean power cycle the first cycle came exactly 10 minutes later and every 5
minutes after that — 11 cycles in the hour, all on schedule. This is a design problem for
installation, not a bench artefact, and the firmware was changed the same day: a BLE
connection now drops the backoff (someone is standing at the logger, so the resets were
human), and the counter is cleared by a measurement that actually ran the sensor rail
instead of by a 10-minute timer, which is what makes the protection work at all.

**2. Keep the phone connection short.** An open BLE link costs more than three times the
sleep current. Connecting for a 5-minute site visit is nothing (≈12 µAh), but an app left
connected for an hour costs as much as three measurement cycles.

## Method note

`--cal` makes single captures usable on their own: the 09-07 measurements needed a zero run
with the DUT lead pulled, and the zero drift between runs was the dominant error (±10 µA).
Here the stored calibration is applied per sample, and with 423k sleep samples the
statistical error is 0.2 µA. The remaining uncertainty is the calibration itself, not the
capture.
